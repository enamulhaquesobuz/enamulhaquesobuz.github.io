# About Me

Hi, I'm MD Enamul Haque Sobuz — a Computer Science student at Guizhou University.
I see the world through two lenses: code and camera.

![My photo](profile.jpg)

By day, I'm learning how the internet actually works — studying Java and Python,
practicing network configuration, and building small projects until they finally
run. I love the logic of technology, and the moment a problem finally clicks.

But I don't live only in front of a screen. I love travel and photography —
for me, they are the same thing. Every trip is a story, and every photo is the
way I keep it. I ride my motorcycle to discover new places, capture streets and
sunsets, and meet people whose lives are different from mine.

My dream? To become a successful businessman someday — but not the kind who
forgets where he started. I want to build things that help people, keep learning
every single day, and one day turn my love for technology and adventure into
something of my own.

In 2026, my short video "Guizhou in My Eyes" won third place in the
Guizhou University International Culture Festival video contest.
